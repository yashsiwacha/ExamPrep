**Auditor / Role:** Head of Growth & Community Marketing Lead

---

## 1. Organic Acquisition Strategy & Philosophy

In the Indian student community, trust cannot be bought through generic Instagram ads; it is earned through **authentic utility, empathy, and peer recommendation**. Competitive exam aspirants actively share tools that give them a tangible edge in syllabus completion or score improvement.

Our zero-dollar growth engine revolves around three pillars:
1. **High-Yield Educational Content (Proof-of-Work):** Solve real syllabus bottlenecks publicly with short-form screen teardowns.
2. **Community Infiltration as a Genuine Contributor:** Participate in high-intent student forums (`r/JEENEETards`, Telegram, WhatsApp) with open-source formula sheets and syllabus roadmaps.
3. **Product-Led Viral Loops:** Students share their Mistake Vault summaries, Mock Test scorecards, and study streak badges directly with peer study groups.

---

## 2. Channel Strategy & Distribution Nodes

### A. Reddit (`r/JEENEETards`, `r/JEEPreparation`, `r/UPSC`)
- **Community Context:** The largest, most candid communities of Indian competitive aspirants (~250,000+ members). Highly allergic to corporate marketing and paid promotions.
- **Tactic (The "Open Tool" Post):**
  - Post Title: *“I built a 100% free, offline study planner & national mock test app because I was tired of messy Notion templates and PW backlog anxiety [No Ads during study]”*
  - Content: Candid founder story, transparent explanation of how the deterministic spaced repetition engine works, link to free Google Play download.
  - Value Give: Include a downloadable PDF checklist of NTA 2026 High-Weightage Chapters created with ExamPrepOS.

### B. YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels (Short-Form Demo Teardowns)
- **Format:** 30–45 second vertical screen recordings showcasing the app in action, accompanied by lo-fi study music and clear voiceover.
- **Pillars:**
  - *"How to complete JEE Physics syllabus in 90 days without burning out"*
  - *"Why you forget formulas after 7 days (and how Spaced Repetition fixes it)"*
  - *"Stop tracking your study in notebooks: Use this offline Android OS"*
- **Call-to-Action:** Link in bio / Search *"ExamPrepOS"* on Play Store.

### C. Telegram & WhatsApp Study Circles
- Target: Regional study channels (Kota, Patna, Delhi Mukherjee Nagar student circles).
- Asset: Free high-yield formula cheat sheets watermarked with:
  > *"Organized via ExamPrepOS — Track your syllabus offline on Android."*
- Zero spam rule: Never blast unsolicited direct messages; share solely in resources channels with moderator consent.

## 3. Product-Led Virality & Share Mechanics

To turn active users into distribution channels without spamming their friends:
1. **The Mock Scorecard Share Card:**
   - After completing a full-length JEE or NEET mock test, the student can export an aesthetically pleasing, dark-mode scorecard graphic (`score, accuracy, time per question, national percentile tier`).
   - Watermark: *"Verified on ExamPrepOS"* with a clean Play Store QR code.
   - Aspirants naturally share these on WhatsApp status and Telegram groups to compare benchmarks with friends.
2. **Study Streak Milestone Badges:**
   - On reaching Day 7, Day 14, and Day 30 streaks, an achievement modal offers 1-tap sharing to Instagram Stories / WhatsApp Status with a minimalist motivational quote.

---

## 4. Ethical Growth Guardrails

- **Zero Shilling / Fake Reviews:** Never purchase fake 5-star ratings or post fabricated student testimonials.
- **Zero Bot Scraping:** Never harvest phone numbers from Telegram or send unsolicited SMS/WhatsApp blasts.
- **Transparent Creator Identity:** Always disclose founder status when posting on Reddit or community forums.
