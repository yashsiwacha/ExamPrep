## 1. Keyword Taxonomy & Mapping

Google Play indexation relies heavily on the **Title (strongest weight)**, **Short Description (second strongest)**, **Package Name**, and natural repetition across the **Full Description (keyword density 1.5%–2.5%)**.

### Tier 1: Primary High-Intent Keyword Clusters

| Keyword Phrase | Search Intent | Strategic Placement | Relevance (1-10) | Competition Density |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `jee main study plan` | High / Direct Problem | Title & Short Description | 10/10 | High |
| `neet syllabus tracker` | High / Direct Problem | Short Description & H2 | 10/10 | Medium |
| `exam preparation app` | Broad / High Volume | Full Description Intro | 9/10 | Very High |
| `pomodoro study timer` | Feature Search | Title / H2 / Bullet 3 | 9/10 | Medium |
| `offline mock test app` | Utility Search | Title / H2 / Bullet 4 | 9/10 | Medium |

## 2. Google Play Store Listing Experiments (A/B Testing Roadmap)

Google Play Console allows running Native Store Listing Experiments without releasing a new APK. We schedule three sequential experiments post-launch:

### Experiment 1: Icon Design (Day 7 – Day 21)
- **Goal:** Maximize Store Listing Impressions to Detail Page Views (CTR).
- **Variant A (Control):** Minimalist Dark OLED icon with modern geometric "Focus Hexagon + Book" emblem.
- **Variant B (Candidate):** Electric Indigo icon with a high-contrast glowing checkmark & lightning focus motif.
- **Hypothesis:** Variant B increases visual prominence in Google Play search results among saturated dark/blue icons, boosting install CTR by +12%.
- **Sample Size Target:** 3,000 visitors per variant (50/50 traffic split).
- **Guardrail Metric:** Day-1 retention (ensure we don't attract low-intent accidental clicks).

### Experiment 2: Screenshot Sequence & Headline Framing (Day 22 – Day 36)
- **Goal:** Maximize Page View to Install Conversion Rate (CVR).
- **Variant A (Feature-First):**
  1. Syllabus Tracker ➔ 2. Focus Timer ➔ 3. Mock Test ➔ 4. Analytics.
- **Variant B (Benefit-First / "Relief Framing"):**
  1. *"Know What to Study Today"* (Hero Dashboard) ➔
  2. *"Never Forget a Formula"* (Spaced Repetition) ➔
  3. *"Authentic National Mocks (+4 / -1)"* (Scorecard) ➔
  4. *"OLED Focus Timer without Ads"* (Timer).
- **Hypothesis:** Benefit-first relief framing addresses student anxiety immediately on the first swipe, lifting CVR by +8–15%.

### Experiment 3: Short Description Angle (Day 37 – Day 51)
- **Variant A:** `Personalized study plans, syllabus tracker, focus timer & mocks for JEE & NEET.`
- **Variant B:** `Stop worrying about your exam backlog. Get daily smart study tasks & mocks.`
- **Hypothesis:** Emotional pain-point messaging ("stop worrying about backlog") drives higher organic install velocity from students suffering from syllabus anxiety.

## 3. Localization Strategy (India Multilingual Expansion)

While English is the primary medium of engineering and high-tier competitive examination prep, Hindi accounts for ~40% of northern state exam queries (UP, Bihar, MP, Rajasthan, Haryana).

### Phase 1: Localized Play Store Listing (Hindi - `hi-IN`)
- Title: `ExamPrepOS: स्टडी प्लान, टाइमर और मॉक टेस्ट`
- Short Description: `JEE और NEET के लिए पर्सनलाइज्ड स्टडी प्लान, सिलेबस ट्रैकर, फोकस टाइमर और मॉक टेस्ट।`
- Localized Screenshots: Hindi headline banners over English in-app screenshots.
- Impact: Unlocks +25–35% organic install reach in Tier-2/3 student clusters at zero incremental engineering cost.
