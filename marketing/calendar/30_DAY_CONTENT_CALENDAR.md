## 1. Content Pillars Architecture

1. **Pillar 1: Systematic Study Planning & Backlog Recovery** — Deconstructing syllabus chaos, daily task clarity, and anti-guilt rebalancing.
2. **Pillar 2: Scientific Spaced Repetition (SRS)** — Beating the Ebbinghaus curve, locking formulas into long-term memory.
3. **Pillar 3: Authentic Mock Test Stamina & Negative Marking** — Test strategy, time allocation per question, palette navigation, score analysis.
4. **Pillar 4: Deep Work & Distraction-Free Study Routines** — Pomodoro presets, OLED battery-saver environment, zero ad interruptions.
5. **Pillar 5: High-Yield Exam Breakdowns & The Mistake Vault** — Why incorrect questions are an aspirant's highest-value asset.
