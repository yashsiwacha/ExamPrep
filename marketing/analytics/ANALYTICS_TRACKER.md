## 1. Asset Attribution & UTM Taxonomy

All published links use standardized UTM parameters to measure exact store listing conversions:

```text
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.examprep.android&referrer=utm_source%3D{platform}%26utm_medium%3D{format}%26utm_campaign%3D{campaign_id}%26utm_content%3D{video_id}
```

### Attribution Examples:
- **YouTube Shorts 01:** `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=shorts&utm_campaign=launch_m1&utm_content=vid01_fatigue`
- **Instagram Reels 01:** `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=reels&utm_campaign=launch_m1&utm_content=vid01_fatigue`
- **Reddit Community Post:** `utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community_post&utm_campaign=launch_m1&utm_content=r_jeeneetards`
